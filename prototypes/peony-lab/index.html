About this prototype

Scripted Blender geometry, rendered in Cycles. Each petal keeps its mesh and UV coordinates while its spine, transverse cup and ruffled surface deform. Layers open on overlapping schedules. Closing displays the exact same poses in reverse.

This is a procedural botanical study, not a scan or a claim of photorealism. Sampled renders preserve lighting and material detail; the player does not crossfade or scale the flower. The camera stays fixed.
