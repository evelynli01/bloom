Hand controls

The camera stays in the bottom-left corner, with the flower name above it. With two hands visible, use one to bloom. Show an open palm with your other hand, then close it into a fist to advance one flower. Reopen your palm to prepare the next change.

Desktop-first prototype. Webcam access is opt-in. All four flowers are stylized, gesture-controlled Canvas illustrations. The flower artwork runs locally. Hand tracking uses MediaPipe and needs internet access to load.
