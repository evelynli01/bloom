Hand controls

The camera stays in the bottom-left corner, with the flower name above it. With two hands visible, use one to bloom. Show an open palm with your other hand, then close it into a fist to advance one flower. Reopen your palm to prepare the next change.

Desktop-first prototype. Webcam access is opt-in. All four flowers are stylized, gesture-controlled Canvas illustrations. The flower artwork runs locally. Hand tracking uses MediaPipe and needs internet access to load.

BLOOM · A quick guide

Step 1 of 3

Start with just one hand.

Either hand works. The first hand the camera sees becomes your bloom hand.

Hand 1 · Bloom

Move only your thumb and index finger.

Move those two fingertips apart to open the flower. Bring them together to close it.

Start with your other hand out of view. Once the flower responds, keep this first hand visible.

Hand 2 · Switch flowers

Open palm → full fist.

  1. Bring your other hand into view, keeping your bloom hand visible.
  2. Face the camera with all five fingers spread. Pause briefly.
  3. Close all five fingers into a fist to move to the next flower.

Open that hand again before each switch. Holding a fist won’t keep changing flowers. A thumb/index pinch is for blooming, not switching.

Hand 1 · Keep blooming

Thumb + index apart ↔ together

Hand 2 · Switch when ready

Open palm → pause → full fist

Keep both hands visible. Your first hand keeps controlling the petals while your other hand changes the flower.

There’s no need to move both hands at exactly the same moment. You can hold your bloom hand steady while switching. Reopen your switching hand to prepare the next change.

Camera access is optional and starts only when you choose to enable it.
